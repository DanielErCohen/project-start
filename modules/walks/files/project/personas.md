# Personas

The cards the `persona-test` skill reads. Each card describes a stranger who tries the product for the first time. Ground it in dated research or real feedback; a card made up from the builder's imagination tests the builder's assumptions.

> ADAPT: write two to four cards for the people who use v1, including one whose situation makes the product hard to use (a small screen, a slow connection, an assistive setting, little patience). Delete the example.

## Card: <first name and one-line role>

- **Who:** age range, work, how comfortable with this kind of product.
- **Goal:** what they came to do, in their words.
- **Device and settings:** the device, browser or terminal, text size, language, any assistive setting.
- **Patience:** how long they wait or retry before giving up, in seconds or attempts.
- **Starts from:** the state of the product on the first screen or command (new account, empty data, seeded data).
- **Basis:** the research, interview or feedback behind the card, with its date.
- **Tasks:** two to four journeys, each with the outcome that counts as done.
