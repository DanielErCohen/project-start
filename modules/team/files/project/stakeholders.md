# Stakeholders

Lenses for the `stakeholder-review` skill: what an outside decider (sponsor, client, regulator, budget holder) has said they want in a document. A lens prepares a draft. It never stands for the person's approval.

> ADAPT: add one lens per person or role that will judge a document, three criteria at most at first, and delete the example. Record observable, work-relevant evidence only: no personal traits, health or motives.

## Lens: <person or role>

- **Basis:** role-derived, or person-specific (stated, observed or inferred).
- **Status:** active, suspended or declined.
- **Recheck by:** a date 30 days out.

| Criterion | Example with its date | Source | Confidence | Counter-evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <the preference as a concrete statement> | <one observable example> | <where it was said or seen> | <high, medium or low> | <anything that contradicts it, or none> |

A recurring preference needs repeated independent examples or a standing statement. A one-off request stays scoped to its meeting. Suspend a criterion at once on a role change, a contradiction, a correction or a request to stop.
