# Module: experiments
Trigger: the project will measure the effect of a change (a test, pilot, price or onboarding trial) or reports results to others.
Adds: `project/experiments.md`.
## Append
- project/brief.md | Each test has a card in project/experiments.md before it starts.
- project/plan.md | A bet that needs a test links to its card in project/experiments.md.
- project/decisions.md | Each experiment card's decision on its read date is logged here.
## Manual
- None.
Skip when: nothing is measured yet or there are too few users for a read. Use counts and interviews until then.
