# Module: ui
Trigger: v1 has users besides the owner, and they use the product through screens or terminal output, or developers use its API.
Adds: `.claude/rules/usability.md`.
## Append
- CLAUDE.md | Screens and output: .claude/rules/usability.md loads on its paths; what a person sees or uses is finished in its own change.
## Manual
- Set `paths` in `usability.md` to the folders holding screens or output, rewrite its user paragraph, and delete rules tagged for surfaces the project lacks.
Skip when: the owner is the only user, or nothing here has a human-facing surface.
